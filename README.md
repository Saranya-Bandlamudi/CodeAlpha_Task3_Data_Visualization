# 📊 CodeAlpha Task 3 – Data Visualization

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed as part of the CodeAlpha Data Analytics Internship.

The dashboard analyzes the Global Superstore dataset to identify important sales trends, regional performance, product performance, and customer segment contributions.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Compare sales across product categories
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Identify monthly sales trends
- Analyze customer segment contribution
- Identify top-performing products
- Provide interactive category filtering
- Present actionable business insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Global Superstore Dataset

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Total Customers KPI
- Average Sale KPI
- Sales by Product Category
- Sales by Region
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Category Filter
- Key Business Insights

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Total sales are approximately 2.26M.
- Technology is the highest-performing product category with approximately 0.83M in sales.
- The West region records the highest sales at approximately 0.71M.
- Consumer customers contribute the largest share of sales.
- Sales show stronger performance toward the end of the year, with December reaching approximately 0.35M.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the highest-selling product in the Top 10 analysis.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into an interactive visual report that helps identify high-performing categories, regions, products, and customer segments for better business decision-making.
